About Coolgc

We are an independent mobile game studio focused on colorful, approachable puzzle experiences for players around the world.

Our story

Small team.
Long-term craft.

Coolgc was founded in Wuhan in 2017. Our team develops and publishes free-to-play games for smartphones and tablets, from bubble shooters and match-3 adventures to merge, sort, jigsaw, and physics puzzles.

We believe a good casual game should be welcoming from the first minute and continue to offer satisfying goals over time. That means clear controls, expressive worlds, thoughtful progression, and regular improvements guided by players.
